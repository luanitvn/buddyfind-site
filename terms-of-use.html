What BuddyFind does

BuddyFind listens for Bluetooth signals to help you find your own earbuds, headphones, watches, trackers and similar devices, and shows their battery levels. It estimates how close a device is from its signal strength. It does not use GPS or show a location on a map.

Use it for your own devices

Use BuddyFind only to find devices you own, or devices you have the owner's permission to find. Do not use it to follow, watch or locate another person, or to find someone else's belongings without their consent. Doing so may be against the law where you live, and it is against these terms.

What it can and can't do

BuddyFind can only hear a device that is switched on, has battery and is sending a Bluetooth signal. It usually can't hear earbuds inside a closed case. Bluetooth reaches about a room to a floor, and walls, bodies and other radio signals change the reading. Battery levels come from the device itself, and some devices don't share them.

Because of this, BuddyFind can't promise that it will find a device, that a reading is exact, or that a low-battery alert will arrive before a device runs out. Don't rely on it where losing a device could cause harm, such as for medical devices or for safety.
