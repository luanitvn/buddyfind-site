BuddyFind Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

BuddyFind is an Android app that helps you find your own Bluetooth earbuds, headphones, watches and trackers, and shows their battery. It is made by JC Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data, in plain words.

The short version

  • BuddyFind has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own.
  • Your saved devices, their battery levels and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • BuddyFind never reads or stores your location.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone.
  • When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics, which works for us. Crash reports contain no device names or Bluetooth addresses. See Crash reports.
  • The app also sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, which works for us. They contain no device names or Bluetooth addresses. See Usage statistics.
  • To keep the hot/cold search accurate, the app downloads updated search settings from Google's Firebase Remote Config. The request contains no device names or Bluetooth addresses. See Search settings.
  • This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

What BuddyFind does on your phone

Listening for Bluetooth signals. While a screen that scans is open (My devices, Nearby, or the hot/cold Find screen), BuddyFind listens for Bluetooth signals around you. For each device it hears, Android gives the app the device's name (if it has one), its Bluetooth address, the signal strength and the kind of device. This includes devices that are not yours, such as a neighbour's speaker. The app uses this to draw the list, the radar and the hot/cold ring. Devices you have not saved are kept only in memory while the screen is open, are never written to storage and are never sent anywhere. BuddyFind does not scan for Bluetooth devices in the background.

Reading battery levels. For devices connected to your phone, BuddyFind reads the battery level that the device reports to Android or through the standard Bluetooth Battery Service. Not every device shares it.

When the app is closed. When one of your saved devices connects, disconnects or reports a new battery level, Android briefly wakes BuddyFind. The app then records the time and battery level on your phone, updates its home screen widgets and, if the battery is low, shows a notification. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Playing a sound. "Play sound" sends a beep to a device that is connected to your phone, over Bluetooth.

What BuddyFind does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account and does not know who you are.
  • It does not read, store or send your location (see "Permissions" for why Android 11 and older ask for it).
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or messages.
  • It does not use your advertising ID.
  • It does not sell, rent or trade any data, and does not use data for advertising.

Crash reports

When BuddyFind crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a service run by Google LLC, so that we can find and fix the problem. A crash report contains:

  • what went wrong in the app's code (the stack trace) and the state of the app at that moment;
  • details about the phone at the time of the crash, such as the model, Android version, free memory and storage, and the app version;
  • a random installation ID created by Crashlytics, used to count how many installations a crash affects. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.

Crashlytics also sends basic session information (for example when the app was opened and which app version was running) so that we can see how many sessions ran without a crash. As with any internet connection, Google's servers also see your phone's IP address when a report is sent.

BuddyFind does not add your device names, Bluetooth addresses or any other Bluetooth identifier to crash reports. Crash reports are not used for advertising. Google processes this data on our behalf, as our service provider, and may process it in the United States and other countries. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days and then starts deleting them from its systems. You can read more in Firebase's privacy information.

We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working to send crash reports. There is no switch for crash reports inside the app in this version. If you do not want them sent, you can uninstall the app.

Usage statistics

BuddyFind uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google LLC, to learn how the app is used, for example how many people finish the intro or how often a search ends with "Found it". It sends:

  • a list of the screens you open and the actions you take in the app (such as starting a search), with the time;
  • a random app-instance ID created by Google Analytics. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account;
  • the phone model, Android version, app version and language;
  • an approximate region (such as the country or city), which Google works out from your IP address. Google states that Analytics does not store the IP address itself.

BuddyFind does not add your device names, Bluetooth addresses or any other Bluetooth identifier to these statistics, and does not collect your advertising ID or your phone's Android ID. Ad storage and ad personalization are switched off. Google Analytics keeps this event data for 2 months and then deletes it. Google processes this data on our behalf and may process it in the United States and other countries. You can read more in Google's information about how it uses data from apps that use its services.

Search settings

BuddyFind can download updated settings for its hot/cold search, such as the signal strength at which the ring turns Warm, Hot or Right here, from Firebase Remote Config, a service run by Google LLC. This lets us make the search more accurate without a new app version. When you open the app, at most once an hour, it asks Firebase for these settings. The request contains:

  • a random installation ID created by Firebase. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account;
  • the app's package name, the version of the Firebase library, the Android version, and your phone's language, country code and time zone;
  • as with any internet connection, your phone's IP address.

The answer is a few numbers that only the search on your phone uses. The request carries no device names, Bluetooth addresses or other Bluetooth identifiers, and nothing about what you search for. If the request fails, the app keeps the settings it already has. Google processes this data on our behalf and may process it in the United States and other countries. You can read more in Firebase's privacy information.

Google Play

You download BuddyFind from Google Play, which Google runs under its own privacy policy. Google Play gives us summary statistics such as the number of installs, ratings and reviews, and crash rates collected by Android from people who chose to share usage and diagnostics with Google. We cannot identify you from these statistics.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we hold almost nothing that could identify you. Crash reports go to Google only so that it can process them for us, as described in "Crash reports". Usage statistics go to Google only so that it can process them for us, as described in "Usage statistics". Requests for search settings go to Google only so that it can answer them for us, as described in "Search settings".

Deleting your data

You are in control of everything BuddyFind stores:

  • Forget one device: open it in My devices and tap Forget. Its entry is deleted, and the helper records about it are cleared automatically the next time the app handles a battery or connection event. A Find widget set to that device keeps its address until you pick another device or remove the widget. The device stays paired in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
  • Delete everything: in your phone's Settings > Apps > BuddyFind > Storage, tap Clear storage.
  • Uninstall the app: this deletes all of BuddyFind's data from your phone.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically by Crashlytics, starting 90 days after they are sent. Because they carry no name or account, we usually cannot tell which reports came from you, but you can still write to us at luan91.official@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

Children

BuddyFind is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Since BuddyFind keeps your data on your phone, you can see, change and delete it yourself as described above. For anything else, contact us at luan91.official@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

BuddyFind's data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. It is not encrypted separately, so protect your phone with a screen lock. Crash reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Search settings are downloaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Changes to this policy

If BuddyFind starts handling data differently, for example when a later version adds ads or new features, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
